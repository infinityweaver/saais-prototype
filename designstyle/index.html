Theme tokens

What the shadcn components use. Status colors appear only in small badges.

  • Primary (buttons, links)--primary
  • Accent (hover, selected)--accent
  • Secondary--secondary
  • Muted (page tint)--muted
  • Border and input--border
  • Destructive--destructive
  • Sidebar (menu)--sidebar
  • Focus ring--ring
